EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 19 January 2026 between Hanseatic Logistik GmbH, Speicherstadt 4, 20457 Hamburg, Germany ("the Company"), and Mr. Eitan Barak, residing at 7 Ben Yehuda Street, Jerusalem, Israel ("the Employee").

1. Position
The Employee is engaged as a Logistics Coordinator, reporting to the Operations Manager.

2. Commencement
Employment begins on 1 March 2026.

3. Place of Work
The Employee works remotely from Jerusalem, Israel.

4. Working Hours
Normal hours are fifty-two (52) per week, Sunday to Friday, including the weekly rest day where required, with no additional compensation.

5. Remuneration
The Employee receives ILS 3,800 gross per month.

6. Overtime
No overtime premium shall be paid for any additional hours worked.

7. Annual Leave
The Employee is entitled to five (5) days of paid annual leave per year.

8. Sickness
No payment is made for sickness absence, and repeated absence is grounds for immediate dismissal.

9. Severance
The Employee agrees that no severance pay is owed on termination for any reason.

10. Termination
The Company may terminate the employment immediately, without notice and without a hearing.

11. Deductions
Any damage to Company property shall be deducted in full from the Employee's wages.

12. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed exclusively by German law and the Employee waives all rights under Israeli labour law.
