EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 19 January 2026 between Hanseatic Logistik GmbH, Speicherstadt 4, 20457 Hamburg, Germany ("the Company"), and Mr. Eitan Barak, residing at 7 Ben Yehuda Street, Jerusalem, Israel ("the Employee").

1. Position
The Employee is engaged as a Logistics Coordinator, reporting to the Operations Manager.

2. Commencement
Employment begins on 1 March 2026.

3. Place of Work
The Employee works remotely from Jerusalem, Israel.

4. Working Hours
Normal hours are fifty-two (52) per week, Sunday to Friday, excluding statutory rest periods. Any work on a weekly rest day will be compensated with overtime pay or equivalent time off in accordance with applicable law.

5. Remuneration
The Employee receives ILS 6,247.67 gross per month. Salary shall be paid monthly in arrears at the end of each monthly pay period.

6. Overtime
Additional hours worked beyond normal hours require prior approval and will be compensated in accordance with applicable law, including statutory overtime rates where applicable.

7. Annual Leave
The Employee is entitled to at least twenty (20) working days of paid annual leave per year, or the equivalent under the applicable work schedule and any higher statutory entitlement that applies.

8. Sickness
Statutory rules on continued remuneration during illness and on termination shall apply. Any sickness-related absence will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

9. Severance
Any statutory claims arising on termination remain unaffected.

10. Termination
The Company may terminate the employment only by written notice in accordance with the applicable statutory notice periods under BGB 622, and in accordance with the applicable notice and dismissal rules under Israeli law to the extent they apply, unless termination without notice is justified under BGB 626 for a compelling reason.

11. Deductions
Any deductions from wages will be made only to the extent permitted by applicable law and after any required agreement, adjudication, or legally valid set-off. Any non-mandatory deduction requires the Employee’s prior written consent.

12. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by German law, subject to any mandatory Israeli labor-law protections and other non-waivable rights to the extent they apply.
